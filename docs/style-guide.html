Interface style guide · rendered

Everything the words sit in

COPY.md governs the words. STYLE-GUIDE.md governs everything else, and this page is that file drawn rather than described — every swatch, specimen and control below is live CSS lifted from src/styles/tokens.css. Nothing here is imported, so the page opens standalone; if a value disagrees with the stylesheet, the stylesheet is right.

Rule one

No radii, no shadows.

Every corner is square and nothing casts a shadow. Fill is spent twice: the main CTA, and the held heart. Everything else is hairlines and type.

Rule two

Colour never carries a state alone.

An error turns a rule accent and says what to do. A selected swatch is ruled and named in the panel above it.

Fixed header, three columns: 1fr auto 1fr. Icons are lucide-react at 1.125rem with stroke-width: 1.25, in a 2.25rem target, at 0.86 opacity — five icon sizes in one band is what makes a header look assembled.

04

Breadcrumbs

src/components/crumbs.tsx · .crumbs in src/styles/commerce.css. One line at eyebrow scale, 0.12em tracking. The separator is a middot at 45% — a chevron is a second glyph weight inside the smallest type on the page.

Eyewear·The story·Arca I

The last crumb is the current page: one step brighter, aria-current="page", and not a link. Hover a link — the crumb moves to --fg-primary and nothing else moves.

  • It never starts with "Home." The wordmark two centimetres above it already goes home.
  • It never wraps. On a phone the last crumb truncates — the end of the trail is where the reader is standing.
  • The whole thing sits in a <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">.
  • Where it belongs: any page reachable from more than two directions. Today that is /shop/[slug] — reached from the story, the index, You may also like, and search — plus /held, which hangs off the desk.
  • Where it does not: the home page, a story page, or /bag. Those are destinations, not depths.

06

The field

Label above, always — a placeholder that vanishes when you type is a label you cannot check your own answer against. Underline only: no box, no fill, no radius. Focus turns the rule --fg-primary, and that is the whole focus treatment.

Rest, focus and optional

Invalid

The rule goes accent and the message says what to do — colour is never the only carrier.

  • Optional is marked; required is not. Most fields are required, so marking them marks everything.
  • Validate on blur, never on keystroke. Telling someone their email is wrong while they are still typing the @ is a scold.
  • Always the right autoComplete and inputMode. A phone field that opens a qwerty keyboard is a bug.

The folding step

src/components/shop/checkout-steps.tsx · .step. The pattern for any surface that asks more than one question. Three states on data-state: open, done, waiting. Flow and rationale live in docs/COMMERCE-FLOWS.md; this is how it looks.

As a guest. No account, no password.

  • Hairline above each step, one below the last. No boxes.
  • The index is mono at eyebrow scale in --fg-quiet. It tells the reader how many questions are left before they read the first one.
  • The head is the whole button, and it carries aria-expanded.
  • An answered step folds to its answer, not to its title repeated.

Pattern

The breaker. A narrow frieze that sits between chapters, the way Jacques Marie Mage closes one block and opens the next. It is the bridge plaque on the frame, the same emblem the logo carries, redrawn as a single hairline: a doorway under a fan, hatched corners, crossed panels. Repeated across the width of the content, nothing else.

  • Source. The bridge plaque, as the logo draws it. Monoline only: every stroke the same weight, no fills, no thick-and-thin from the logo lockup.
  • Build. The emblem alone. No rails, no rules, no ornaments between. Only whole emblems: the spacing flexes so the row starts and ends on a full one. Stroke stays 1px.
  • Size. Small: 12px tall, about 17px wide, roughly 2px apart. It is texture, not an illustration; it should read as a line from a step back and as the emblem only up close.
  • Opacity. 30% of paper on ink, 30% of ink on paper. Never above 40%. Quieter than the quietest text beside it.
  • Width. Spans the margin: exactly the content column, gutter to gutter, the same edges the text aligns to. Never full bleed, never narrower than the column.
  • Place. Between chapters, with equal space above and below. One per break, never inside a card, never over an image.
  • Orientation. Horizontal and upright. The doorway always stands on the bottom rail.
  • Motion. None. It is a breath between sections, not a ticker.

11

Accessibility floor & mobile

Not a phase. A change that breaks one of these is not finished.

  • Everything reachable by keyboard, with a visible :focus-visible. Never outline: none without a replacement in the same rule.
  • One <h1> per page. On the buy page it is the name in the offer panel — the opening's centred copy is decorative and aria-hidden.
  • Every icon-only control has an aria-label. Every decorative image has alt="".
  • A toggle carries aria-pressed; a disclosure carries aria-expanded and aria-controls; a panel carries role="region" and aria-labelledby.
  • An overlay locks the page with overflow: hidden and scrollbar-gutter: stable, or every fixed element jumps by the scrollbar's width.
  • prefers-reduced-motion is honoured everywhere, including in self-animating SVGs.
  • 44px minimum target. .nav-icon is 36px of box around an 18px glyph in a 24px-padded band, which clears it; nothing smaller ships.
  • No horizontal overflow, ever. The breadcrumb truncates rather than scrolls.
  • The buy row pins to the foot of the screen and takes itself away once the film has been scrolled past. A sticky element that covers content is worse than no sticky element.
  • Test at 375px before calling anything done.
